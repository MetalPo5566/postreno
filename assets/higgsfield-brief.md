# Image brief: postreno.kleaner.my

Seven named slots under `public/images`, all **1040 x 780, 4:3**. Drop a file in
at the same name and the page picks it up with no code change. The share card
at `public/og/index.jpg` is 1200 x 630 and is generated by
`npm run assets`, not here.

The Kleaner logo is already in Higgsfield as media id
`2d60467a-b3fb-4efa-af6a-b640cc5ac738`. Use it as the reference for the uniform
so the blue reads as ours rather than as generic workwear.

## Rules that apply to every prompt

- Photoreal. No illustration, no 3D render, no stock-photo gloss.
- **No legible numbers on any meter or display.** The screen may glow, it may be
  angled away, it may be slightly out of focus. It may never show a figure. A
  made up reading on a page whose whole argument is measurement would undo the
  page.
- **No text baked into the image.** No signage, no labels, no watermarks.
- Brand blue accents only where they belong: the uniform, a piece of equipment.
  Nothing tinted blue for the sake of it.
- Malaysian context: KL condo and landed interiors, tropical daylight, local
  faces. No European or North American interiors.
- Natural light wherever possible. Mid morning, windows open, no flash.
- **No generated before and after pairs.** See the note at the bottom.
- No video anywhere on the page. The performance budget does not allow it.

## The slots

### 1. `postreno-hero.jpg` (the LCP image, the only preloaded file)

> A freshly renovated living room in a Kuala Lumpur condominium, completely
> empty of furniture, shot in soft mid morning light from a large window. New
> floor to ceiling built-in cabinets in pale timber laminate along one wall. A
> thin, even film of cement dust across the new floor tiles, catching the light.
> One Kleaner cleaner in a blue uniform stands mid frame holding a small
> handheld air quality meter at chest height, looking at it. The meter screen is
> angled away from camera and is not legible. Photoreal, wide angle but not
> distorted, natural colour, no text anywhere in frame.

### 2. `protocol-1.jpg` Dust Extraction

> A cleaner in a blue uniform running an industrial HEPA vacuum across dusty new
> floor tiles in an empty, newly renovated Malaysian apartment. Visible cement
> and gypsum dust on the untouched part of the floor, a clean track behind the
> vacuum head. Daylight from an uncurtained window. Photoreal, no text in frame.

### 3. `protocol-2.jpg` Deep Clean

> Close in shot of a cleaner in a blue uniform wiping the inside of a newly
> installed kitchen cabinet with a microfibre cloth. Drill dust and sawdust
> visible in the corner of the cabinet interior. New cabinet carcass, hinges and
> shelf pins clearly new. Shallow depth of field, natural light, photoreal, no
> text in frame.

### 4. `protocol-3.jpg` Formaldehyde Treatment

> A technician in a blue uniform holding a handheld air quality meter close to
> the open door of a newly built plywood wardrobe in an empty bedroom. The
> wardrobe interior shows raw plywood and MDF edges. The technician is looking
> at the meter. **The meter display is not legible from this angle.** Calm,
> clinical but domestic, photoreal, natural light, no text in frame.

### 5. `who-family.jpg`

> A young Malaysian family, two parents and a small child, standing together at
> the open front door of their new, completely empty home, looking into it.
> Photographed from inside the unit, so they are lit from behind by daylight
> from the corridor. Warm, quiet, not posed or grinning. Photoreal, no text in
> frame.

### 6 and 7. `postreno-before.jpg` and `postreno-after.jpg`

**Do not generate these.** They drive the drag slider, which is a proof device.
A generated pair would be two different rooms pretending to be one, which is the
one thing that section cannot be.

They need a real pair from a real job, shot from the **same position, the same
height and the same focal length**, once before the team starts and once after
they finish. Lock the camera to a tripod mark and do not move it.

Until that pair exists the gradient placeholders stay, and the caption under the
slider says so in as many words.

## Generating

```bash
npm run assets     # rebuilds fonts, logo, placeholders and the OG card
```

To drop real photos in, overwrite the file at the same name, keep the 4:3 crop
at 1040 x 780 or larger, then `npm run build`. Nothing else needs editing.

They are **not** in this repository. The machine that built this site routes

CDN host (`d8j0ntlcm91z4.cloudfront.net`) by organisation policy, the same way
it refuses `kleaner.my`. The files could be created but not fetched back, so the
gradient placeholders are still what ships.

### To put them in

1. Open each link below, or find the generation in the Higgsfield workspace.
2. Check it against the rules at the top of this file, in particular that **no
   meter reading is legible** in slots 1 and 4. Regenerate any that fails.
3. Save it, crop or resize to **1040 x 780**, save as JPEG at quality 82.
4. Drop it into `public/images/` at exactly the name in the table.
5. `npm run build`. Nothing else needs editing.
